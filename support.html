Support

How can we help?

Answers to the common questions are below. For anything else, email support@benvenutisoftware.com and you'll hear back from the developer.

How do I let Heads Up see my meetings?
On first launch, Heads Up asks for Calendar access. Choose Allow. If you missed it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars and turn on Heads Up. Access is read-only; Heads Up never changes your calendar.
Which calendars does it work with?
Any calendar in the macOS Calendar app, including iCloud, Google, Outlook, and Exchange accounts. Add accounts in System Settings → Internet Accounts and Heads Up sees their events.
A meeting isn't showing up. Why?
Heads Up only reminds you about real meetings, so it skips events that are all-day, that you've declined, that are marked "free," or that run longer than four hours (offsites and travel blocks). Any other timed event on your calendar appears.
How do the reminders work?
By default you get a reminder card five minutes before a meeting and a full-screen reminder the moment it starts. Choose which reminders fire (15, 10, 5, or 2 minutes before, and at start) in Preferences → Reminders. Esc dismisses a reminder and Return joins.
What does Auto-join do?
Press Auto-join on a reminder card and Heads Up closes the card, then opens the meeting link for you when the meeting starts. Auto-join only appears before a meeting starts; after that, Join does the same thing.
Which meeting links can it open?
Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex links, plus other links in the invite. Click Join to open them.
How do I make it launch automatically?
Open Preferences → General and turn on Launch when I log in.
Does it collect my data or use the internet?
No. Heads Up reads your calendar on your Mac to show your next meeting and does nothing else with it. There's no account and no tracking, and no data leaves your Mac. The only network activity is your browser opening a meeting link when you click Join. See the privacy policy.
