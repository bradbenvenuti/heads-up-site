Privacy policy · Updated October 22, 2026

Heads Up collects nothing.

It doesn't collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. This page explains exactly what it does touch.

What Heads Up accesses

Heads Up asks for read-only access to your calendar so it can show your next meeting and remind you before it starts. It reads event details (start and end times, titles, and any meeting link) on your Mac, to display them to you. That is the only information it uses.

What Heads Up does not do

  • It does not collect or store your calendar data anywhere outside your Mac.
  • It does not send your data to us or to any third party.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry.
  • It has no account and no sign-in.
  • It makes no network requests, with the one exception below.

The one time it touches the network

When you click Join, or when Auto-join opens a meeting for you, Heads Up hands that meeting's link to your default browser or app (for example Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or Webex). That connection is made by your browser to the meeting provider, not by Heads Up to us. Heads Up itself sends nothing.

Your data stays on your Mac

All calendar information Heads Up reads stays on your device and is never uploaded. Your preferences, such as which reminders are on, are stored locally on your Mac. There is no cloud service behind Heads Up.

This website

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies, and it doesn't track you across sites or build a profile of you. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address when you load a page.

Children

Heads Up is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
