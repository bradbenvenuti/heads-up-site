Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Heads Up is built to respect your privacy completely. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information — full stop.

What Heads Up accesses

Heads Up requests read-only access to your calendar so it can show your next meeting and remind you before it starts. It reads event details — start and end times, titles, and any meeting link — entirely on your device, to display them to you. That is the only information it uses.

What Heads Up does not do

  • It does not collect or store your calendar data anywhere outside your Mac.
  • It does not send your data to us or to any third party.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry.
  • It has no account and no sign-in.
  • It makes no network requests, with a single exception below.

The one time it touches the network

When you click Join on a meeting, Heads Up opens that meeting's link in your default browser or app (for example, Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or Webex). That connection is made by your browser to the meeting provider — not by Heads Up to us. Heads Up itself sends nothing.

Your data stays on your Mac

All calendar information Heads Up reads remains on your device and is never uploaded. Your preferences (such as which reminders are enabled) are stored locally on your Mac. There is no cloud service behind Heads Up.

Children

Heads Up is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
