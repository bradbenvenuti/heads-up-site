What happens before each meeting

Heads Up reads the calendars already on your Mac. You don't set anything up per meeting.

  1. All dayIn the menu bar

    A ring that fills as the meeting gets closer

    One glance tells you how long you have. Click it to see the rest of your day, with a Join button for the next meeting.

  2. −5:00Or 15, 10, or 2 minutes. You choose

    A reminder card with a live countdown

    It appears with a chime. Join now, dismiss it, or press Auto-join and Heads Up opens the meeting link for you the moment it starts.

  3. 0:00The meeting starts

    Your whole screen, until you act

    The reminder dims everything behind it, on every monitor if you like, and stays until you join or dismiss. No banner to swipe away on reflex. Esc dismisses, Return joins.

The details

Finds the meeting link
Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex links, or any other link in the invite.
Works with your calendars
Anything in the macOS Calendar app: iCloud, Google, Outlook, and Exchange.
Skips what you're not attending
All-day events, declined invites, and "free" blocks never trigger a reminder.
Handles double-booking
When two meetings start together, one reminder lists both, each with its own Join.
Starts when you log in
Turn on "Launch when I log in" once and it's there every morning.
Light, dark, or quiet
Follows your Mac's appearance. Turn the sound or the animation off in Preferences.

Private by design
  • Your calendar stays on your Mac. Access is read-only.
  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics.
  • No network requests of its own. The only thing that goes online is your browser, when you click Join.

I built Heads Up because I kept missing meetings. Not from not caring: I'd get deep into something and look up too late. Every reminder I tried was too easy to ignore, so I made the one I needed, a countdown I can glance at and a reminder that takes over the screen when it matters. It's a one-time purchase because I didn't want to charge you every month to be reminded of your own meetings.
