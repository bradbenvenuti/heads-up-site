THE MEETING RADAR

Your day, at a glance.

A quiet ring in your menu bar fills as your next meeting approaches. One glance tells you how long you have — no clock math, no anxious tab-switching to your calendar.

Open the dropdown for the full day: what's next, what's later, and how much room you have in between.

REMINDERS THAT WORK

Impossible to miss.

A gentle card slides in before the meeting. When it starts, Heads Up takes over your whole screen — every monitor, if you like — until you join, snooze, or dismiss.

Choose exactly which reminders fire: 15, 10, 5, or 2 minutes before, and at start. Press Esc to dismiss any of them.

STRAIGHT INTO THE CALL

One click to join.

Heads Up finds the meeting link buried in the invite — Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, or any other link — and puts a Join button everywhere a reminder appears.

Every calendar you use

Works with anything in the macOS Calendar app — iCloud, Google, Outlook, and Exchange accounts.

Only real meetings

All-day events, declined invites, and "free" blocks are filtered out, so reminders only fire for meetings you'll actually attend.

Always on duty

Turn on "Launch when I log in" once, and Heads Up is quietly watching your calendar every time you sit down.

Private by design.

Heads Up reads your calendar on your Mac to find your next meeting — and does nothing else with it. The only network activity is your browser opening a link when you click Join.
