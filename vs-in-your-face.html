Heads Up vs In Your Face

Two apps built on the same idea: a meeting reminder you can ignore is useless. Here's an honest look at how they differ.

Both Heads Up and In Your Face put a full-screen reminder over your work when a meeting starts. If that's what you're after, either app does it. The real differences are the price, the privacy model, and how many platforms you need.

Price: pay once vs keep paying

Heads Up

One-time purchase. Updates included.
In Your Face
$3.99/mo
Or $24.99 a year, or $69 one-time. Free trial available.

Heads Up costs less than one year of In Your Face's subscription, and their one-time license is about 3.5 times the price. There's no renewal to remember: you buy it once and keep it.

Privacy: local calendar vs connected accounts

In Your Face can connect directly to your Google or Microsoft account to read your calendar. Heads Up never touches the network: it reads the calendars already in your Mac's Calendar app (iCloud, Google, Outlook, Exchange) with read-only access, and nothing leaves your Mac. No account, no sign-in, no tracking. If your employer is wary of third-party apps holding calendar-account access, that difference matters.

Which one should you get?

Choose In Your Face if…

  • You need reminders on Windows, iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch. Heads Up is Mac-only.
  • You use a niche meeting tool. Their link detection covers 30+ services.
  • You want to customize themes and alert sounds.

Choose Heads Up if…

  • You work on a Mac and want to pay once: $19.99, no renewal.
  • You don't want another app holding a connection to your calendar account. Heads Up is local and read-only.
  • You want a countdown in the menu bar, not just the alarm at the end.
