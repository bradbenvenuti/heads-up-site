Heads Up vs In Your Face

Two apps built on the same conviction: a meeting reminder you can ignore is useless. Here's an honest look at how they differ.

Both Heads Up and In Your Face put a full-screen, impossible-to-miss reminder over your work when a meeting starts. If that's what you're here for, either app solves it. The real differences are the price model, the privacy model, and how many platforms you need.

Price: pay once vs keep paying

Heads Up costs less than a single year of In Your Face's subscription — and if you compare buyouts, their lifetime license is 3.5× the price. There's no trial math to do and no renewal to remember: you buy it once and it reminds you of meetings until you retire.

Privacy: local calendar vs connected accounts

In Your Face connects directly to your Google or Microsoft account to read your calendar. Heads Up never touches the network: it reads the calendars already in your Mac's Calendar app — iCloud, Google, Outlook, Exchange — with read-only access, and nothing leaves your machine. No account, no sign-in, no tracking. If your employer frowns on third-party apps holding calendar-account access, that difference matters.

Which one should you get?

Choose In Your Face if…

  • You need reminders on Windows, iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch — Heads Up is Mac-only.
  • You live in a niche meeting tool — their link detection covers 30+ services.
  • You want to customize themes and alert sounds heavily.

Choose Heads Up if…

  • You work on a Mac and want to pay once — $19.99, no renewal, ever.
  • You don't want another app holding a connection to your calendar account — Heads Up is local and read-only.
  • You want a glanceable countdown in the menu bar, not just the alarm at the end.
