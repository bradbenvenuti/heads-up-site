Meeting reminder for Mac

The meeting reminder for Mac that's hard to miss.

Heads Up is a native macOS menu bar app. It counts down to your next meeting and takes over your whole screen the moment it starts, so you're on the call on time without watching the clock.

Built for the Mac, not ported to it

Heads Up lives where a Mac utility should: in your menu bar, quiet until it's needed. A ring fills as your next meeting approaches, and a click opens a native menu with your day and a one-click Join button. It reads the calendars you already have in the macOS Calendar app: iCloud, Google, Outlook, and Exchange.

What it does

Menu bar countdown
A ring shows how long until your next meeting. One glance, no math, no switching to your calendar.
Reminders before the meeting
A full-screen reminder with a live countdown appears 15, 10, 5, or 2 minutes before, whichever you choose.
Full-screen reminder at the start
When the meeting begins, Heads Up takes over your entire screen (every monitor, if you like) until you join or dismiss.
Auto-join
Not ready to switch yet? Press Auto-join on the reminder and Heads Up opens the meeting for you the moment it starts.
One-click join
Finds Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex links (plus other links) in the invite.
Only real meetings
Skips all-day events, declined invites, and "free" blocks.

Private by design

Heads Up reads your calendar entirely on your Mac. There's no account, no sign-in, and no tracking, and it makes no network requests of its own. The only time anything touches the network is your browser opening a meeting link when you click Join. Calendar access is read-only; it never changes your calendar.
